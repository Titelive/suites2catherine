Place Stanislas

Completed in 1755, a UNESCO World Heritage site since 1983. Jean Lamour’s gilded gates, café terraces and, on summer nights, the “Rendez-vous” light show.

Also worth a visit

  • Museum of Fine Arts

    From Perugino to Delacroix, the Daum collection and Jean Prouvé.

    Closed Tuesdays

  • Musée lorrain

    The Ducal Palace, Georges de La Tour and Callot’s Beggars.

    Closed Mondays and Tuesdays

  • Villa Majorelle

    Nancy’s first house built entirely in Art Nouveau.

  • Musée de l’École de Nancy

    The essential stop for Nancy’s Art Nouveau.

  • Museum-Aquarium

    Aquariums and a natural history gallery in an Art Deco building.

    Closed Mondays

  • Iron History Museum

    The Eiffel Tower’s iron was cast here.

    Jarville, closed Tuesdays

  • Jean-Marie Pelt Botanical Garden

    12,000 species and 2,500 m² of tropical greenhouses.

    Villers-lès-Nancy

  • Parc de la Pépinière

    The big city-centre park.
